## 🚀 Featured Projects

### 🧠 [Private PDF Chatbot (RAG)](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/RAG-Private-pdf-chatbot)
A fully local chatbot that answers questions from your PDFs with page-level citations. Documents never leave your machine.
- Page-aware chunking, vector search with Qdrant, and local LLMs through Ollama
- Query rewriting, streaming answers, and an evaluation harness (retrieval hit rate, faithfulness, latency)
- Benchmarked three local models and picked the one that ran about 2.7x faster on limited hardware

`Python` `FastAPI` `Streamlit` `Qdrant` `Ollama` `Docker`

### 🚗 [License Plate Recognition](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/License_plate_recg)
Detects license plates in uploaded videos and reads their text.
- YOLOv11 detection fine-tuned on a Roboflow dataset (50 epochs, 640 px), with EasyOCR text recognition
- Samples video frames and returns unique plates with confidence scores and timestamps
- Results stored in MySQL, with CSV export and a Flask web interface for uploading videos and reviewing past analyses

`Python` `Flask` `OpenCV` `YOLOv11` `EasyOCR` `MySQL`

### 📈 [Crypto Risk Management and Prediction](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/crypto-risk-management-and-prediction)
Analyzes cryptocurrency market trends and financial risk, with a dashboard to track multiple currencies.

`Python` `Data Analysis` `Visualization`

### 🌿 [Plant Disease Diagnosis System](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/Plant_disease_diagnosis_system)
Identifies plant diseases from leaf images and suggests remedies.
- Deep learning model built with TensorFlow and Keras, organized as a modular pipeline for data loading, training, evaluation, and prediction
- Web interface (HTML, CSS, JavaScript) where users upload a leaf photo and get the diagnosis with disease information and recommended solutions
- Multilingual support in English, Hindi, Urdu, and Kannada to make it usable for local farmers

`Python` `TensorFlow` `Keras` `HTML/CSS/JS`

### 📝 [Whiteboard Scanner](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/White-board-scanner)
Detects and straightens a whiteboard in a photo, enhances it, and extracts the text, with a WER/CER evaluation pipeline.

`Python` `OpenCV` `RapidOCR` `Streamlit`

### ❤️ [Heart Risk Prediction](https://github.com/syedamanals86-debug/Heart_risk_prediction)
A machine learning model that predicts heart disease risk.

`Python` `Machine Learning`
